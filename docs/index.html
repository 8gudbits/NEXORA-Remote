Nexora

Wireless Slide Remote

Use your phone to control a presentation's slides on your computer. No mobile app, no cables.

On your computer
  1. 1 Download the NEXORA app from the link below.
  2. 2 Open it - A window with a 6-digit code and a QR code will appear.
Download NEXORA App

(Windows only - double-click to run)

On your phone

You have two easy ways to connect:

Open Remote Manually

Then type the 6-digit code from the computer screen.

— or —

Scan the QR code with your phone's camera (or any QR code app) and tap the link that appears.

Now you can use remote buttons on your phone to control the slides.
